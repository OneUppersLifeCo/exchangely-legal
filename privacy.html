Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 26, 2026

Introduction

Exchangely is a currency converter mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Exchangely you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

Exchangely does not require an account and does not collect personally identifiable information directly. Your preferences (selected currencies, base currency, decimal places, auto-update toggle) and recently fetched exchange rates are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences and Hive. They never leave your device unless you explicitly export them.

Network requests

To provide live exchange rates, the app fetches data from a third-party rate provider (ExchangeRate-API). Only the requested base currency code is sent in the request URL. No personal data is transmitted.

Third-party services

A future release of Exchangely will include advertising provided by Google AdMob and AppLovin (mediation). These services may collect anonymized identifiers and usage data per their own privacy policies. You will be asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any personalized advertising data is collected, in accordance with GDPR.

Permissions

Exchangely requires only Internet access, used solely to fetch live exchange rates. No camera, location, microphone, or contacts permissions are requested.

Children

Exchangely is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.

Contact
